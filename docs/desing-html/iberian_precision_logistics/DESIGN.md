## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an architectural, high-trust digital identity for premium enterprise freight, expedited delivery, and supply-chain logistics. Merging classical corporate reliability with agile European tech precision, the aesthetic couples deep architectural obsidian blacks and warm tactile sand neutrals with an energetic magenta/rose signal accent.

### Design Movement & Aesthetic Direction
- **Architectural Corporate Modernism**: Rooted in precision grids, crisp delineations, and tactile warmth. Avoiding sterile monochrome enterprise tropes by using warm Iberian sand and alabaster foundations.
- **Tone & Character**: Authoritative, frictionless, swift, and exact. It inspires effortless control over mission-critical supply routes, high-value cargo, and real-time transit telemetry.
- **Contrast Dynamics**: Extreme contrast pairs (deep black text and structural framing over warm stone surfaces) punctuated by high-visibility rose magenta for immediate status identification, live tracking, and dispatch alerts.

## Colors

The palette is derived directly from the physical identity: intense industrial obsidian, calibrated warm stone substrates, and a high-frequency transit magenta that commands visual priority without degrading corporate poise.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#111111`)**: Deep Solid Obsidian. Governs structural elements, primary actions, heavy typography, and prominent structural chrome.
- **Secondary (`#E6D5C3`)**: Architectural Sand/Beige. Provides structural grounding, boundary dividers, active container states, and neutral surface shading. Paired with `#CBB499` for refined borders and subtle accents.
- **Tertiary (`#EE5283`)**: Signal Rose Magenta. Reserved for live dispatch telemetry, high-priority SLAs, real-time map waypoints, active shipment tags, and focused focal highlights. Complemented by `#E11D48` for pressed states.
- **Neutral Base (`#F7F3EE`)**: Alabaster Sand. The primary canvas substrate, mitigating cold eye fatigue across heavy data tables and route manifests while ensuring crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) cards retain physical presence.

### Functional Logistics Tokens
- **Canvas / App Shell**: `#F7F3EE`
- **Surface Elevation (Cards/Containers)**: `#FFFFFF`
- **Border / Divider Soft**: `#E6D5C3`
- **Border / Structural Hard**: `#CBB499`
- **Status - On Schedule / Active**: `#111111`
- **Status - In Transit / Critical Route**: `#EE5283`
- **Status - Neutral Cleared / Stored**: `#8C827A`

## Typography

The platform utilizes **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies to ensure high geometric legibility, clean horizontal scanning, and an approachable yet strictly corporate posture.

### Typographic Rhythm & Telemetry Data
- **Waybill & Tracking Identifiers**: Rendered using `label-mono-tracking` in uppercase with expanded tracking (`0.08em`) to guarantee zero ambiguity between alphanumeric references (e.g., `ES-MAD-9842X`).
- **Data Densities**: Metrics, cargo volumes, and customs timestamps use tabular figures (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`) to ensure strict column alignment across high-frequency tables.
- **Editorial Contrast**: Pair large, dark bold headlines (`headline-lg`) against delicate muted captions to establish immediate informational structure.

## Layout & Spacing

A strictly regimented 12-column responsive layout anchors the dispatch operations, route planners, and shipment consoles. 

### Breakpoints & Grid Mechanics
- **Desktop (>= 1280px)**: 12-column layout, max-width `1440px`, `margin-lg` (3.5rem), `gutter-lg` (2rem). Complex split-screen views (waybill telemetry list on the left 4 columns, interactive live route tracking map on the right 8 columns).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px)**: 8-column layout, `margin` (2rem), `gutter` (1.5rem). Tables convert to horizontally scrollable viewports with locked primary identifier columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout, `margin-sm` (1rem), `gutter-sm` (1rem). Dense dashboards compress into stacked modular cards with expanded touch targets.

### Spacing Scale Rules
- Maintain vertical cadence using multiples of 4px/8px.
- Compact inner card padding (`space-md`) is standard for high-density manifest data tables, while external card margins utilize `space-lg` to prevent visual fatigue.

## Elevation & Depth

This system avoids heavy drop shadows in favor of **structural boundaries and warm tonal layering**, reminiscent of high-end tactile stationery combined with contemporary European editorial design.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Foundation Level (Canvas)**: `#F7F3EE`. The ground layer upon which all modules sit.
2. **Elevated Surface (Cards, Modules, Sidebars)**: Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) bordered with a precise 1px rule in `#E6D5C3`. Zero blur shadow during static rest states.
3. **Hover & Focus Elevation**: Subtly lifted with an ambient, warm-tinted shadow: `0 12px 28px -6px rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.06), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(203, 180, 153, 0.15)`. Border transitions to `#CBB499`.
4. **Floating Overlays (Modals, Context Menus, Dispatch Tooltips)**: Pure `#FFFFFF` surface with a crisp dark structural keyline (`1px solid rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.08)`) and high-diffuse shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(17, 17, 17, 0.14)`.
5. **Interactive Highlight**: High-priority waypoint nodes and real-time vehicle badges utilize a faint magenta ring halo (`0 0 0 4px rgba(238, 82, 131, 0.18)`).

## Shapes

The interface balances sharp industrial discipline with modern ergonomic software standards by deploying calibrated corner radii.

### Shape Geometry Application
- **Standard Cards & Module Enclosures**: `rounded-xl` (1rem / 16px). Creates a cohesive, modern container silhouette that softens dense tabular metrics.
- **Major Dashboards & Modal Canvases**: `rounded-2xl` (1.5rem / 24px). Framing large operational interfaces, map containers, and delivery timeline views.
- **Controls & Form Elements**: `rounded-md` to `rounded-lg` (0.5rem - 0.75rem / 8px - 12px) for inputs, selects, and standard action buttons to keep active hit areas crisp and clearly defined.
- **Status Pills & Tracking Chips**: Full pill geometry (`rounded-full` / 9999px) to distinctly contrast against the rectangular structural geometry of cards and grids.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Dispatch / Confirm Shipment)**: Solid `#111111` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-lg`, subtle internal 1px border. Hover transitions to `#262626`. Focus ring is a 2px offset ring in `#EE5283`.
- **Secondary Action (Export Route / Filter)**: `#FFFFFF` background, `#111111` text, 1px border in `#CBB499`. Hover transitions background to `#F7F3EE`.
- **Accent / Critical CTA (Track Live Express)**: `#EE5283` background, `#FFFFFF` text, bold weight. Hover transitions to `#E11D48`.
- **Destructive / Cancel Route**: Muted transparent red tinted with `#111111` text and red border.

### Status Pills & Logistics Badges
- **In Transit (Active)**: Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#FDF2F4` background, `#EE5283` border and text, featuring a 6px pulsing dot indicator in `#EE5283`.
- **Delivered (Completed)**: `#F7F3EE` background, `#111111` text, `#CBB499` border.
- **Customs Hold / Delayed**: Solid `#111111` background, `#F7F3EE` text, high-contrast urgency.
- **Priority Indicator**: Ultra-compact uppercase pill (`label-mono-tracking`), 2px vertical padding, 8px horizontal padding.

### Data Tables (Freight & Manifests)
- **Header**: `#F7F3EE` background, uppercase `label-mono-tracking`, `#8C827A` color, 1px border-bottom in `#E6D5C3`.
- **Row Styling**: Alternating `#FFFFFF` to subtle `#FDFAF7` row striping or full white with 1px `#E6D5C3` dividers. Row hover triggers an immediate `#F7F3EE` tint and transforms the left border into a 3px vertical accent bar in `#EE5283`.
- **Numeric & Waybill Cells**: Tabular numbers, right-aligned for weight/volume, left-aligned for origin/destination route badges.

### Input Fields & Search Bars
- **Tracking Search (Primary Hero Input)**: Deep inset or floating white container with a 1.5px border in `#CBB499`. Leading search/parcel icon in `#8C827A`. Focus shifts the border to `#111111` with a soft `#EE5283` 3px focus shadow.
- **Form Inputs**: Compact 44px height, `#FFFFFF` surface, placeholder text in `#8C827A`.

### Cards & Route Timeline
- **Shipment Overview Card**: `#FFFFFF` background, 1px solid border in `#E6D5C3`, `rounded-xl`, inner padding `space-lg`.
- **Timeline Milestones**: Vertical 2px line in `#E6D5C3`. Completed waypoints marked with solid `#111111` checkpoints; the active transit hub is marked with an expanded ring in `#EE5283` enclosing a white node.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Crisp 18px squares/circles, `#FFFFFF` fill, 1.5px border in `#CBB499`. Checked state transitions to `#111111` with crisp white iconography or `#EE5283` for urgent multi-selection manifests.
